### Theseus

[![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3.x-blue?logo=python)](https://www.python.org/)

**Python · Research & OSINT Assistant**

Theseus is a Python-based research assistant for structured information retrieval and OSINT-oriented analysis.

The project explores how software can support investigative research by transforming research questions into structured queries, retrieving relevant information, applying deterministic analysis, and maintaining persistent research state.

#### Core Focus

* Structured information retrieval
* OSINT-oriented research workflows
* Deterministic data analysis
* Research state and evidence management
* Reproducible investigative workflows

Theseus is primarily a learning and research project focused on the intersection of **computer science, information retrieval, data analysis, and investigative research**.
